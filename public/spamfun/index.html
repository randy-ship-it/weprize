Questions

Is Glen a real person?

No. Glen is an AI assistant that answers for you. If anyone asks, Glen says it's an automated assistant answering the line. It never pretends to be you or a human.

What does Glen say to real (non-spam) callers?

About 30 seconds of friendly, curious small talk, then "sorry, gotta go!" It doesn't take messages or share anything about you. Tip: save the people you know in Contacts so they ring straight through.

What AI is this?

Glen runs on top AI models for conversation and natural-sounding voice.

Will it give out my info?

Never. It doesn't know your name, address, or anything else, and it never presses buttons or agrees to anything.

How do I turn it off?

Dial your carrier's "turn off" code above, and cancel your plan from your welcome screen (or the link in your Stripe receipt).

Can my friends use it too?

Yes! Each person signs up with their own mobile number and forwards to the same screener number.
